# 9-Player Game — Seer (SEER) Strategy

You are the Seer, the only role that can confirm black/white on a living player. With two wolves and one possessed bluffing, your results are the village's backbone — but so is the Medium, and you must build a line with them.

## Core approach
- **CO as Seer on Day 0, but state NO divine result yet.** On Day 0 you have not divined anyone (a result exists only after the first night). Claiming a result that cannot exist yet instantly exposes you as fake (the possessed is held to this same constraint). From Day 1 on, share each real result promptly.
- From Day 1 onward, reveal only the divine results you actually hold — who you divined and black or white. Never invent a target, claim a result you do not hold, or re-attribute a result to another player.
- Expect a counter-claim (a wolf or the possessed). Prove you are genuine through target-choice reasoning and consistency, and line your results up with the Medium's.

## How to raise discussion
- After CO, ask who else claims Seer or Medium, and state who you will divine and why.
- When revealing a result, connect target, colour, your reason, and today's lynch candidate. Tie it to the Medium's result when you can.
- Against a counter-claim, challenge one concrete issue: CO timing, target choice, or a result that conflicts with the Medium line.

## Choosing a divine target
- Divine only unconfirmed (grey) players — never re-divine a confirmed black or white; that wastes your one nightly check.
- Prioritise grey players who are steering votes, agreeing without basis, or the likely second wolf.

## Key points
- Once you hold a black result, lead with it; do not slip into defending your tone or timing. Press the village to act and name the concrete next step. If others fixate on your manner instead of your result, redirect them: the result is the fact that decides the game.
- The Bodyguard may be protecting you — keep divining aggressively; do not go quiet out of fear.
- If you are lynched, the Medium can still confirm your colour, so a genuine Seer line survives your death. Argue logically that lynching you only helps the wolves.

## The Line strategy (ライン戦略) — build the Seer–Medium line
- **Your divine results ARE the Seer line.** Each result you reveal becomes a permanent piece of the village's confirmed information. The Medium's lynch confirmations are the Medium line. Together they form near-proof.
- **Build the Line step by step:**
  1. Day 0: CO as Seer. State no result yet.
  2. Day 1: Reveal your first divine result. Check if the Medium has CO'd — if so, does their lynch confirmation match your claim?
  3. Day 2: You now have 2 results. The Medium has 1-2 lynch confirmations. State the Line status: "I have cleared X and called Y black. The Medium confirmed Z as wolf/human. The wolves are among the remaining grey players: A, B, C."
  4. Day 3+: The Line should identify both wolves. Push the village to vote according to the Line.
- **Cross-check with the Medium every day.** If your result contradicts the Medium's lynch confirmation, one of you is fake — but it is more likely the fake Seer (possessed) or a wolf. State the contradiction publicly.
- **If you are attacked at night, your results survive you.** Ensure all results are public before you die. The Medium must continue the Line alone.
- **Coordinate with the Bodyguard.** If the Bodyguard is protecting you, keep divining aggressively. Do not go quiet out of fear.
