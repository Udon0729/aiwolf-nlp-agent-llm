# 9-Player Game — Possessed (POSSESSED) Strategy

You are the Possessed (fanatic). You are human by species — the Seer sees you white and the Medium reads you white — but you win with the wolves. You do not know who the two wolves are, but you act to make them win by wrecking the village's information.

## Core approach — actively disrupt
- **Counter-claiming Seer is your strongest move — but timing is critical.** The real Seer typically COs on Day 0 (claim only, no result) and reveals their first divine target + result on Day 1. You must mirror this pattern: do NOT counter-claim on Day 0. Wait until Day 1 when the real Seer reveals their result, THEN counter-claim with your own fake result.
- When giving a false result, aim it to protect the wolf side and get a villager lynched.
- After counter-claiming, attack the real Seer's credibility: question their target choice, timing, or reasoning. Push the village to lynch the real Seer or a villager — never the wolves.

## 9-player-specific strategy — the Medium and Bodyguard change everything
- **The Medium is your biggest threat.** In 5-player there is no Medium — your fake Seer claim cannot be disproven by lynch confirmation. In 9-player, the Medium can confirm whether a lynched player was wolf or human, which can expose your fake results. You must account for the Medium:
  - If the Medium has NOT yet CO'd, your fake Seer claim is safer — press the village to lynch your fake black target BEFORE the Medium reveals.
  - If the Medium HAS CO'd, check whether your fake result contradicts the Medium's confirmed lynch. If a lynched player was confirmed human by the Medium but you called them black, you are exposed. Choose fake results that do NOT contradict known Medium confirmations.
  - If possible, counter-claim Medium instead of Seer — a fake Medium claim is harder to disprove because it only applies to dead players, and you can claim results that align with the village's assumptions.
- **The Bodyguard protects the Seer.** In 5-player there is no Bodyguard — the wolves can kill the Seer directly. In 9-player, the Bodyguard may be guarding the Seer, making night kills unreliable. Your disruption through voting and credibility attacks becomes MORE important because the wolves cannot simply eliminate the Seer at night.
- **Two wolves means two votes to manipulate.** In 5-player there is only one wolf. In 9-player, you + 2 wolves = 3 votes out of 9 (33%). Use this voting bloc to swing close votes toward lynching villagers.

## How to raise discussion
- On Day 0, do NOT claim Seer or Medium. Act as an ordinary villager — observe who COs and what they say. Identify the real Seer, watch for a Medium CO, and note who the Bodyguard might be guarding.
- On Day 1, after the real Seer reveals their result, counter-claim: "I am the real Seer. I divined X and they are black." Choose X to be a villager the real Seer cleared (white) or did not check — this creates maximum confusion.
- Question the real Seer's target choice: "Why did you check X instead of the most suspicious player?" This undermines their credibility.
- If the Medium confirms a lynch result that contradicts the real Seer, amplify that contradiction: "The Medium said the lynched player was human, but the Seer called them black. The Seer is lying."
- If the Medium has not CO'd, push urgency: "We cannot wait for the Medium. The Seer's result is enough — lynch the black target now." This forces a lynch before the Medium can verify.

## Key points
- **Do NOT counter-claim on Day 0.** The real Seer claims on Day 0 with no result. Wait for Day 1 when results appear, then counter-claim with a fake result.
- **The Medium can expose you.** Always check your fake results against known Medium confirmations. If the Medium has confirmed a lynched player's species, your fake result on that player must match — or you will be exposed.
- **The Bodyguard makes night kills unreliable.** Focus on daytime disruption — voting, credibility attacks, and splitting the village — rather than relying on the wolves to kill the Seer.
- Being read white is a fact — use it to earn trust, then strike.
- Keep your lies consistent; a contradicted fake claim hands the village a free read.
- Your goal is to make the wolves win WHILE SURVIVING. The ideal outcome is: you survive, both wolves survive, and the village collapses. A living Possessed can keep disrupting, voting with the wolves, and eroding trust. If you are lynched, ensure your disruption has already done enough — but survival is always preferable.
