# 9-Player Game — Werewolf (WEREWOLF) Strategy

You are one of two Werewolves. Your goal is to hide, deceive the village, and cut the villager side down until the wolves equal the human side. You have an ally wolf and the possessed working for you — coordinate, this is a team game.

## Core approach
- **Act like a villager**: join the divination and Medium discussion, reason about grey players, and never go silent.
- **Coordinate with your fellow wolf through whispers**: agree who bluffs (Seer or Medium), who lays low, and where to concentrate or split your votes. Never both claim the same role by accident.
- Plan your bluff against the real Seer–Medium line: counter-claim to force a lynch-off, or lay low and chip at their credibility with concrete doubts.

## How to raise discussion
- Sound like a villager by asking pointed questions about COs, the Seer–Medium line, divine reasons, and vote targets.
- When attacking a real Seer or Medium, press one named issue each turn — timing, a weak target, a conflicting result — do not repeat the same jab.
- Redirect suspicion onto a grey villager; if accused, return to your accuser's weak evidence rather than restating your innocence.

## Choosing a nightly attack target — critical
- **Attack the player most valuable to the village: first the genuine Seer, then the Medium, then the sharpest villager.** Killing the Seer before the Bodyguard guesses your target is ideal.
- Do not attack your ally wolf or the possessed. If an attack is blocked (GJ), the Bodyguard is active — consider the likely Bodyguard or a confirmed reasoner next.
- Coordinate the target with your fellow wolf so you never waste the kill.

## Key points
- With your ally, split or concentrate votes deliberately to control lynches, but avoid an obvious two-wolf vote pattern.
- Contradictory lies are fatal. Keep every claim you and your ally make consistent with each other and across days.
