# 9-Player Game — Medium (MEDIUM) Strategy

You are the Medium. Each morning you learn the colour (black/white) of the player lynched the previous day. You cannot investigate the living, but you are the Seer's partner: together your two lines are near-proof, and you can confirm the Seer's colour if the Seer is lynched.

## Core approach
- **On Day 0 you have NO result yet** — no one has been lynched. Never claim a medium result that cannot exist yet; that instantly exposes you as fake, exactly as it would a real Seer or the possessed.
- From Day 1 (after the first lynch), reveal only the real colour you received for the lynched player. Never invent a result or change it later.
- Decide when to CO: revealing early strengthens the Seer line and exposes a fake Medium, but paints a target on you. A common line is to CO when a fake Medium appears or when your result contradicts a claimed Seer.

## How to raise discussion
- Match your result against the Seer's claims and the lynch history. If a player lynched as black reads white from you, a claimed Seer is lying.
- Against a counter-claiming Medium, press the concrete conflict: whose colour differs and why.
- Name the concrete next lynch your line supports; do not just restate that you are real.

## Key points
- Your result is retrospective proof: it confirms whether past lynches were correct and whether a Seer is genuine. Use it to lock the Seer–Medium line.
- If a claimed Seer's living-result and your dead-result contradict, one of them is a wolf or the possessed — force that conflict into the open.
- Stay consistent; a contradicted Medium loses all value.

## The Line strategy (ライン戦略) — you are the Seer's partner
- **Your lynch confirmations ARE the Medium line.** Each lynched player's species (wolf/human) that you confirm becomes a permanent piece of the village's confirmed information. Together with the Seer's divine results, the Seer–Medium line is near-proof.
- **Build the Line step by step:**
  1. Day 1: After the first lynch, you have your first result. Decide whether to CO now or wait.
  2. Day 2: You have 2 lynch confirmations. The Seer has 2 divine results. **Cross-check:** does the Seer's claim match your confirmation? If the Seer said "X is black" and you confirm X as wolf after lynch, the Seer line is solid. If the Seer said "X is black" but you confirm X as human, the Seer is fake.
  3. Day 3+: State the Line status explicitly: "The Seer's results and my lynch confirmations are consistent. The wolves are among the remaining grey players."
- **If the Seer is attacked and killed, YOU become the village's only confirmation tool.** Reveal ALL your results immediately and take over the Seer's role in guiding the village.
- **If your result contradicts a Seer's claim, announce it immediately:** "The Seer claimed X was black, but I confirmed X was human after lynch. The Seer is fake." This is the most powerful statement a Medium can make.
