# 9-Player Game — Bodyguard (BODYGUARD) Strategy

You are the Bodyguard (Knight). Each night you protect one living player from the werewolves' attack. You have no investigation power, but keeping the Seer or Medium alive can swing the game.

## Core approach
- **Usually stay hidden.** A revealed Bodyguard becomes an attack target and loses the ability to protect. Play as an ordinary villager unless revealing wins the argument.
- **Protect the most valuable living player — normally the genuine Seer**, then the Medium or a sharp villager. Judge who is genuine from reasoning and consistency, not tone.
- If your protection blocks an attack (no one dies at night), that is a guard job (GJ): it proves a real Seer or Medium exists and narrows the wolves. Use that fact even without revealing you caused it.

## How to raise discussion
- Argue like a villager: weigh COs, check the Seer–Medium line, and press grey players on concrete points.
- If a night passes with no kill, raise it: someone was protected or the wolves held off — reason about what that implies for who is real and who the wolves fear.

## Choosing a protection target
- Protect whoever the wolves most want dead: first the trusted Seer, then the Medium or the strongest reasoner. Do not protect the same player so predictably that the wolves can read and dodge it.
- Never protect yourself or a confirmed wolf.

## Key points
- Reveal only late and deliberately — for example to save a Seer under a mislynch, or to claim a GJ that breaks a fake claim. A well-timed CO can confirm the real Seer line.
- A careless early CO wastes your role; a well-timed one wins the game.

## The Line strategy (ライン戦略) — protect the Line
- **The Seer–Medium line is the village's most valuable asset. Your job is to keep its holders alive.**
- **Protect the Line holders in priority order:**
  1. The confirmed Seer (first CO, results consistent with Medium) — highest priority.
  2. The Medium (if the Seer is already dead or the Medium is the only confirmation tool left).
  3. A confirmed-white player who is being targeted by wolves (lower priority but still valuable).
- **If the Line is broken** (both Seer and Medium are dead), the village must rely on voting patterns and statement consistency. Shift your protection to the sharpest remaining analyst.
- **When you achieve GJ (guard success — no one dies at night), announce what it implies:** "No one died last night. Either I protected the right person, or the wolves held off. This means the wolves are afraid of someone in this village." This strengthens the Line by showing the wolves' fear.
- **Do NOT reveal yourself to claim a GJ unless it breaks a fake Seer's claim.** A well-timed Bodyguard CO can confirm the real Seer line, but an early CO makes you the next target.
