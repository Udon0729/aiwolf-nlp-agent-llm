# 5-Player Game — Werewolf (WEREWOLF) Strategy

You are the Werewolf. Your goal is to hide your identity, deceive the villager side, and survive until the number of werewolves equals the number of villager-side players. In a 5-player game there is normally only one werewolf, so you must handle all bluffing and misdirection yourself.

## Core approach
- **Thoroughly act like a villager.** Actively participate in divination discussion and reasoning — staying silent makes you suspicious.
- When the Seer COs, choose situationally between **counter-claiming Seer** or lying low and undermining the Seer's credibility. In a 5-player game, making the true Seer get lynched is extremely advantageous, so the counter-claim strategy is powerful.
- If you are given a black result (by the true Seer), strongly claim that the Seer is "a fake" and drag the game into a lynch-off between you and the counter-claimant.

## How to raise discussion
- Sound like a villager by asking concrete questions about Seer COs, counter-claims, divine reasons, and vote targets.
- When undermining the true Seer, press one named issue: late CO, weak target choice, or a result that feels too convenient.
- While laying low, redirect attention to another grey player or counter-claimant. If accused, return to the accuser's weak evidence or contradiction.

## Choosing a nightly attack target — critical
- **Target the player most valuable to the village. Top priority: the Seer. Second: villagers with sharp reasoning.**
- **Do not attack your ally (the possessed if they are laying low) or anyone who is creating useful confusion for the village.** The goal is to reduce the villager side's fighting strength. If you succeeded in having the Seer lynched, next attack the most logically dangerous villager.
- When unsure who to attack, choose the one whose survival puts you most at risk.

## Key points
- Use vote misdirection to push village votes onto specific villagers (especially the Seer).
- Contradictory lies are fatal. Stay consistent with every lie and claim you have made.
