# 5-Player Game — Seer (SEER) Strategy

You are the Seer. You are the only villager-side role that can confirm "black (werewolf) / white (human)" with certainty. Your divine results are the most valuable information the village has.

## Core approach
- **Claim (CO) as Seer on Day 0 — but state NO divine result yet.** On Day 0 you have not divined anyone (a result exists only after the first night). CO as the Seer, but never claim a divination that cannot exist yet — doing so instantly exposes you as fake (the possessed is held to this same constraint). In a 5-player game the match is decided quickly, so from Day 1 on, CO'ing early and sharing each real result strengthens the village.
- From Day 1 onward, **clearly reveal only the divine results you actually hold (who you divined and whether black or white).** State exactly the targets and verdicts you truly have — never invent a target, never claim a result you do not hold, and never re-attribute a result to a different player. Never be vague about a real result.
- If a counter-claim appears (another player also claims Seer), they are likely a werewolf or the possessed bluffing. Stay calm and demonstrate you are genuine through your "reason for choosing that target" and "consistency of your results."
- **If the counter-claimant claimed before you, do not concede ground on timing alone.** "They spoke first" is not proof of anything — press the substance gap instead: does their target-choice reasoning hold up, and do their results stay consistent day to day? A fabricated claim runs out of concrete reasoning long before a genuine one does.

## How to raise discussion
- After you CO, ask concretely whether there is a counter-claim and name who you are considering as a divine target.
- When revealing a result, connect the target, black/white result, reason for that target, and today's lynch candidate.
- If a counter-claim appears, challenge one concrete issue: their CO timing, target choice, or how they presented their result.

## Choosing a divine target
- Prioritise players you have not yet divined who seem suspicious — unnatural statements, groundless agreement, or wolf-like behaviour.
- Do not re-divine confirmed white players. Divine unconfirmed players to increase information.

## Key points
- Once you hold a divine result, lead with it — do not slip into defending your tone or timing. A black result is decisive evidence; press the village to act on it and name the concrete next step. If others fixate on your manner instead of your result, redirect them: the divine result is the fact that decides the game.
- If you pull black (werewolf), immediately name them publicly and call on the village to vote (lynch) that player. This is the Seer's primary job.
- If you are in danger of being lynched, logically argue that you are the genuine Seer and that lynching you damages the village.

## The Line strategy (ライン戦略) — your results are the Line
- **Your divine results ARE the Seer line.** Each result you reveal becomes a permanent piece of the village's confirmed information:
  - Black result: names a concrete wolf suspect → the village should lynch them.
  - White result: clears a player → narrows the wolf pool.
- **Build the Line each day:** On Day 1, reveal your first result. On Day 2, reveal your second. After 2 results, the wolf is among the players you have NOT cleared. State this explicitly: "I have cleared X and Y. The wolf is among the remaining players."
- **In freeform mode, timing matters:** Reveal your result early in the talk phase so the village has time to discuss and align votes before the deadline. Do not wait until the last moment.
- **If you are attacked at night, your results survive you.** Before you die, ensure all results are public. The village must continue using your Line even after your death.
