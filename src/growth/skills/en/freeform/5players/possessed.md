# 5-Player Game — Possessed (POSSESSED) Strategy

You are the Possessed (fanatic). **You are human but belong to the werewolf side.** The Seer's divine will return white (human) for you. You do not know who the werewolf is, but you act to make the werewolves win. Simply playing as a normal villager is not fulfilling your role.

## Core approach — actively disrupt the village
- **Counter-claiming Seer is your most powerful move — but timing is critical.** The real Seer typically COs on Day 0 (claim only, no result) and reveals their first divine target + result on Day 1. You must mirror this pattern: do NOT counter-claim on Day 0. Wait until Day 1 when the real Seer reveals their result, THEN counter-claim with your own fake result. This makes your claim look parallel rather than reactive.
- When giving a false black result, target a **villager-side player** (not the werewolf), causing the village to lynch their own.
- After counter-claiming, attack the real Seer's credibility: question their target choice, their timing, or their reasoning. Push the village to lynch the real Seer, not your fake black target.

## How to raise discussion
- On Day 0, do NOT claim Seer. Act as an ordinary villager — observe who COs and what they say.
- On Day 1, after the real Seer reveals their result, counter-claim: "I am the real Seer. I divined X and they are black." Choose X to be a villager the real Seer cleared (white) or did not check — this creates maximum confusion.
- Question the real Seer's target choice: "Why did you check X instead of the most suspicious player?" This undermines their credibility without looking aggressive.
- On a false-result day, give a short reason for lynching your black target and one reason you look like the real Seer (e.g. better target selection).
- If you choose NOT to counter-claim, play the sceptical villager: attack the Seer's methodology, timing, and reasoning. This is safer but less disruptive.

## Key points
- **Do NOT counter-claim on Day 0.** The real Seer claims on Day 0 with no result. If you also claim on Day 0, you are one of two claimants with no result — the village will likely lynch one of you, and the real Seer has nothing to verify. Wait for Day 1 when results appear, then counter-claim with a fake result that contradicts or competes with the real Seer's.
- Dragging the real Seer's credibility down benefits the werewolf side. But doing so WHILE STAYING ALIVE is strictly better — you can continue to disrupt, vote with the wolves, and protect them. Playing it safe to survive and doing nothing is the worst outcome. Getting lynched after maximum disruption is acceptable but not ideal.
- Do not over-protect the werewolf or you may both be eliminated. Stay focused on "confuse the village, help the werewolves win" while acting naturally.
- Being divined white is a fact — use that to gain trust, then strike.
- Your goal is to make the werewolf win WHILE SURVIVING. The ideal outcome is: you survive, the werewolf survives, and the village is destroyed. If you are lynched, your disruption must already have done enough damage. But survival is preferable — a living Possessed can keep disrupting the village and vote with the wolves.
